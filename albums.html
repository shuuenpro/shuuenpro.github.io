A doujin CD that acts sort of like a one-shot (i.e. first draft) album for Shuuen. Includes the original IA versions of the first four songs (except NND / final version of D-ne's song) and 2 new songs.

Shuuen -Re:write- (終焉-Re:write-)

(Released 2013/03/13) (2 discs, 27 + 5 tracks)

First major album of Shuuen! Focuses on the Occult Club. Includes four new songs + unique Vocaloid covers of the Occult Club's songs + Underdog Supremacy, Common Heroes, and a remaster of Bookmark of Demise. Features narration by Aizawa Mai and Kamiya Hiroshi.

Has an additional piano arrangement mini-album by Suzumu.

Shuuen -Re:mind- (終焉-Re:mind-)

(Released 2015/03/25) (19 tracks)

Second major album of Shuuen! Focuses on the Film Club. Includes 13 new songs, including the character-focused songs of the Film Club. Only one song has received an official MV. Features narration by Kamiya Hiroshi.

There are two store-exclusive CDs associated with the limited edition: Shuuen -Re:take-, which features two self-covers, and Shuushin -Re:clining-, which features a discussion between 150P and Suzumu.
